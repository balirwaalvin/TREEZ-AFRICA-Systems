Our history

Company Changelog

The milestones that shaped TREEZ AFRICA SYSTEMS, in order.

2019

The beginning

TREEZ AFRICA HOLDINGS was founded with a vision to create a technology-driven ecosystem across Africa. The Systems division was established as the core engineering arm.

2020

First major project

Delivered our first enterprise banking solution for a regional bank, processing over 100,000 transactions daily and establishing our expertise in financial technology.

2021

Mobile revolution

Launched our mobile development division, delivering 15+ mobile applications that reached over 200,000 users across East Africa during the digital acceleration of 2020.

2022

Enterprise expansion

Expanded into enterprise systems for healthcare, logistics, and agriculture. Grew the team past 50 specialists, partnered with Google Cloud and Microsoft, and launched a dedicated Windows application development practice.

2024

Innovation hub

Launched an innovation hub focused on AI, blockchain, and IoT. Crossed our 100th delivered project, and part of the team was recognized as GitHub Campus Experts.

2026

Today & beyond

150+ projects delivered, 80+ active clients, and a growing footprint across East Africa. Still shipping — still based in Kampala.
