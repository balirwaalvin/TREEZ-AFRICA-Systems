Our Journey

The TREEZ AFRICA Story

From a bold vision to a leading technology company, here's how we got here.

2019

The Beginning

TREEZ AFRICA HOLDINGS was founded with a vision to create a technology-driven ecosystem across Africa. The Systems division was established as the core tech arm.

2020

First Major Project

Delivered our first enterprise banking solution for a regional bank, processing over 100,000 transactions daily and establishing our expertise in financial technology.

2021

Mobile Revolution

Launched our mobile development division, delivering 15+ mobile applications that reached over 200,000 users across East Africa and beyond during the digital acceleration of 2020.

2022

Enterprise Expansion

Expanded into enterprise systems for healthcare, logistics, and agriculture. Grew our team to over 50 specialists and opened partnerships with Google Cloud and Microsoft. We as well launched the Windows Application Department dedicated to Windows Application Development

2024

Innovation Hub

Launched our innovation hub focused on AI, blockchain, and IoT solutions. Completed our 100th project milestone and partnered with GitHub to have part of our team as GitHub Campus Experts.

2026

Today & Beyond

With over 100+ projects delivered and 80+ satisfied clients, we continue to push boundaries and shape the digital future of Africa through cutting-edge technology solutions.
