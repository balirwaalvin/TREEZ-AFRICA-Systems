FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to questions we get asked the most

How much does a typical project cost?

Project costs vary depending on scope, complexity, and timeline. A simple web application may start from UGX 11,000,000, while enterprise banking systems can range from UGX 185,000,000 to UGX 1,850,000,000+. We provide free consultations and detailed quotes tailored to your specific requirements.

How long does it take to build a software product?

Timelines depend on project complexity. A basic web app can be delivered in 4-8 weeks, a mobile app in 8-16 weeks, and enterprise systems in 3-12 months. We use agile methodologies to deliver working features early and iterate based on your feedback.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support?

Absolutely! We offer flexible maintenance and support packages including 24/7 monitoring, bug fixes, security patches, performance optimization, and feature updates. We believe a product launch is just the beginning of the journey.

Can you work with our existing development team?

Yes! We offer team augmentation services where our engineers integrate seamlessly with your existing team. We also provide consulting, code reviews, and architecture guidance to help your internal team deliver better results.

What industries do you serve?

We serve a wide range of industries including banking & fintech, healthcare, education, agriculture, logistics, e-commerce, government, and telecommunications. Our team brings domain expertise to each sector, ensuring solutions that meet industry-specific requirements and regulations.

Do you sign NDAs and protect intellectual property?

Yes, we take confidentiality very seriously. We're happy to sign NDAs before any project discussions. All intellectual property developed during a project belongs to you. Our contracts clearly define IP ownership and confidentiality terms.

Where are you located? Can you work with international clients?

We are headquartered in Kampala, Uganda, and serve clients across Africa and internationally. We have experience working with remote clients using modern collaboration tools — Slack, Jira, GitHub, Zoom, and more. Time zone differences are managed through overlapping work hours and asynchronous communication.
